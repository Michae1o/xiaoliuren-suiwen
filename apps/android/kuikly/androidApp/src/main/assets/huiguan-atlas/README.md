# Song dynasty atlas data used in 回观

`song-1052.json` is an independently licensed data asset (CC BY-SA 4.0). It is not a photograph or tracing from 《星空帝国》, not a complete atlas, and not a live-sky model.

- Star patterns and mansion anchors: Sun Shuwei (孙殳玮), [Stellarium Chinese Song Dynasty Sky, v26.2](https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/blob/v26.2/skycultures/chinese_song_dynasty/description.md) ([original data](https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/blob/v26.2/skycultures/chinese_song_dynasty/index.json)).
- Star positions, motions and magnitudes: David Nash / Astronomy Nexus, [HYG v4.1](https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database/blob/main/hyg/README.md).
- Adaptations: 28 principal asterisms, the six walls of the Three Enclosures, Northern Pole and Northern Dipper; joined by HIP identifiers (231 stars); 1052 approximate mean-equator frame from IAU 1976 precession and rectilinear stellar propagation; polar azimuthal-equidistant projection. The astronomical north pole is a separate coordinate marker, not an extra star. The mansion boundaries run along their anchors' right-ascension directions.
- The data and its derived star-chart artwork are shared under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The report interface and user records are separate from the atlas dataset.
